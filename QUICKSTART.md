# Quick start

**Puck Peak Legacy Updater** brings the rosters of *NHL Legacy Edition* (PS3, played on RPCS3, or
the PC port NHL Legacy Native) up to today: NHL, European leagues, AHL, juniors and national teams. A free community project
from [Puck Peak](https://www.puckpeak.com).

## Main features

- **Today's NHL rosters** from NHL.com: every player on his team, at his NHL.com position, with
  numbers, lines and captains. Utah, Seattle and Vegas included.
- **Current player ratings** in every league.
- **Real clubs and players** in Liiga, Extraliga, SHL, DEL, National League, Norway, the AHL and
  the OHL, QMJHL and WHL.
- **National teams** refreshed, and every empty one filled.
- **Photos, logos and real team names** in the game's menus (also on the favourite-team screen),
  with one click to restore the game's own pictures.
- **EU and NA** versions of the game, or both at once.
- **No roster needed:** start from a community roster, or from the game's own roster on your disc.
- **Roster editor:** look at any team, edit players and teams, its lines, strategy and jerseys, and
  keep your edits for every later update.
- **Calendar:** the real 2026-27 season month by month, and the results of your own Season or Be a GM.
- **Front office:** each NHL team's payroll, cap space and contracts; change a contract yourself.
- **Real free agents and drafts:** retired players gone, unsigned NHL players there, every player's
  real draft, and draft prospects on real clubs where the game's draft finds them.
- **Windows and Linux**, with RPCS3 or with just a folder of roster saves; on Windows also with NHL Legacy Native.
- **Safe:** an update is saved only after it passes a safety check, and the roster it changes is first
  copied to a backup folder (or choose **Save as a new roster** and nothing existing is touched).

## How to use it

1. Download from [Releases](https://github.com/MatusRiedl/puckpeak-nhl-legacy-updater/releases/latest):
   **`PuckPeakLegacyUpdater.exe`** on Windows, **`...-linux.tar.gz`** on Linux (unpack it, start
   `PuckPeakLegacyUpdater` in the folder). The program is not signed: on Windows choose *More info > Run anyway*.
2. **Close the game** if you want photos and logos.
3. **Setup tab, "Your game":** pick the file `rpcs3.exe` in your RPCS3 folder (on Linux the program
   finds RPCS3 by itself), or `NHLLauncher.exe` of NHL Legacy Native. Neither on this computer? Tick
   **Export files to a folder** and pick the roster file (README, "Without RPCS3").
4. **Setup tab, "Which roster do you work on?":** pick the roster to start from. *The game's own
   roster* is at the end of the list if you have none yet.
5. **Update tab:** leave everything switched on, or switch off what you don't want. Under **Save**,
   choose **Update this roster** or **Save as a new roster**.
6. Press **Update roster**. It takes a few minutes. The result shows what changed, and
   **List of changes** opens every change, team by team.
7. In the game: start it. If the updated roster is not the one in use, **Customize > Save/Load/Delete > Load >
   Rosters**, pick it, then **save the roster once** so the game keeps it. (A new roster is named
   with today's date and time.)

## Good to know

- New players get a generic face, and no commentary name.
- The game has a fixed number of teams and player places. A few clubs without a slot, and some
  junior depth players, are left out; the list of changes names them.
- Already updated once? Just update again from your newest roster: only what changed is redone.
- The window has ten tabs: **Setup**, **Game patch** (32 teams and the year 2026 in Season and Be a GM,
  optional), **Update**, then **Roster**, **Lines**, **Strategy**, **Jerseys & ice**, **Calendar** and
  **Front office**, which share one team list on the left, and **Draft & Pro** (the next drafts' prospects and your
  own Be a Pro player). Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 and Ctrl+0 open a tab.
- Your own pro: make him in **Draft & Pro** > My pro, press **Save**, then in the game pick him in Live the Life >
  New Pro > Be a NHL Pro (his team, his position, step the Overall filter). The game's other New Pro starts make a
  player in the game itself.
- A Be a Pro career you play: **Draft & Pro** > Be a Pro careers, pick it, change your pro's name, his ratings or the
  experience he has to spend, and press **Save this career** with RPCS3 closed (a backup copy is made first).
